Free tool · Companies House data

How old is this company, and is it in good standing?

Enter a company name or number. You get its exact age, its status, whether accounts and the confirmation statement are overdue, any insolvency history, and previous names.

Free, no sign-up. Uses your own Companies House API key (Settings). See a worked example

Useful for

A quick check before you pay an invoice, sign a supplier, or quote a new customer. Overdue filings and recent name changes are worth a second look.

Where the data comes from

The company profile on the Companies House register, read live through its free API. Status and filing dates update when Companies House processes a filing, usually within a day.

What “up to date” means

Next accounts and confirmation statement deadlines are not in the past. It says nothing about whether the figures are healthy; that needs the accounts themselves.

Limits

Covers companies, LLPs and limited partnerships on the UK register. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships are not registered at Companies House.
