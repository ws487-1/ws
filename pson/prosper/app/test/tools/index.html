Quick answers from the Companies House register

Three small tools for the checks people make every day before doing business with a UK company. Each reads the official public register directly, shows where every fact comes from, and keeps nothing about you.

Director lookupSearch the Companies House officers register for a director and see all their current and past appointments in one list.Company age & status checkerCheck when a UK company was incorporated, whether it is active, and whether its accounts and confirmation statement are up to date.Registered office lookupList every company with its registered office at a UK address or postcode, active and dissolved, and spot formation-agent addresses.

Honest about the data

Everything comes from the Companies House public register under the Open Government Licence. We do not guess, scrape other sites or add data we cannot source. Where we combine records, we say so.

Your key, your browser

Searches run in your browser with your own free Companies House API key, saved only in this browser. Without a key you can still see a worked example of each tool.
