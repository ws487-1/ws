See every company a director is, or was, involved with

Type a name to search the official officers register. Companies House often holds several records for the same person, so we group records with the same name and birth month, then list current and past roles side by side.

Free, no sign-up. Uses your own Companies House API key (Settings). See a worked example

Useful for

Checking who you are about to do business with, spotting a history of dissolved companies, or finding a founder’s other ventures before a call.

Where the data comes from

The Companies House public register, read live through its free API with your own key. Records are as filed by the companies; Companies House does not verify that two records are the same person, so “likely the same person” is our grouping, not theirs.

What we leave out

Home and correspondence addresses, and day of birth. Companies House publishes month and year of birth only; we show it solely to tell people with the same name apart.

Limits

Name search returns the closest 30 matches. Very common names may need a middle name. Appointments are capped at 50 per record.
