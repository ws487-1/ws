Which companies are registered at this address?

Enter an address with its postcode. We list every company whose registered office is there, active first, and tell you when it looks like a shared address used by an accountant, formation agent or virtual office.

Free, no sign-up. Uses your own Companies House API key (Settings). See a worked example

Useful for

Checking whether a supplier really operates from the address on its invoice, finding the other businesses in a building, or spotting addresses linked to many short-lived companies.

Where the data comes from

Companies House advanced search, matched on the postcode and then on the building number you type. Read live through the free API with your own key.

A registered office is not a trading address

Many small companies use their accountant’s or a virtual office’s address. A long list here usually means a service address, not a large business.

Limits

Shows the first 500 companies at a postcode. Addresses typed differently on filings (for example “Flat 2” and “2”) may need the postcode-only search.
